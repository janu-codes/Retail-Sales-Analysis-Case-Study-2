# AICT Case Study 2 – Retail Sales Analysis

## Problem Statement
A retail company sells products across three categories: Electronics, Clothing, and Groceries. The objective is to analyze monthly sales, identify the category generating the highest revenue, and understand sales trends for business planning.

## Objectives
- Analyze sales from January to June.
- Calculate monthly and category-wise sales.
- Apply spreadsheet formulas and data analysis techniques.
- Create charts to visualize sales trends and distributions.
- Interpret results and recommend business improvements.

## Dataset
The dataset contains six months of sales values in Indian rupees for Electronics, Clothing, and Groceries.

## Spreadsheet Functions
SUM, AVERAGE, MAX, MIN, IF, COUNTIF.

## Data Analysis
Monthly totals, category totals, sorting, filtering, conditional formatting, and category-wise summaries.

## Visualizations
1. Line chart – Monthly sales trends
2. Bar chart – Category sales comparison
3. Pie chart – Category contribution
4. Histogram – Electronics sales distribution
5. Scatter plot – Month versus Electronics sales
6. Heat map – Sales performance

## Key Observations
- Electronics generated the highest revenue: ₹4,80,000.
- June recorded the highest monthly sales: ₹2,10,000.
- Electronics sales increased from ₹50,000 in January to ₹1,10,000 in June.
- A bar chart is most appropriate for comparing product categories.

## Conclusion
Sales increased consistently from January to June. Electronics contributed the largest share of revenue. The company should plan inventory around increasing demand, review category performance regularly, and use sales trends to guide future decisions.
