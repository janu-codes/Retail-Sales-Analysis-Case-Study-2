# Part E – Interpretation

**1. Which product category contributes the highest sales?**  
Electronics, with ₹4,80,000 in revenue (about 51.6% of total sales).

**2. Which month records the highest sales?**  
June, with total sales of ₹2,10,000.

**3. What trend is observed in Electronics sales?**  
Electronics sales consistently increase from ₹50,000 in January to ₹1,10,000 in June, an overall rise of 120%.

**4. Which visualization is most appropriate for comparing product categories?**  
A bar chart, because it makes differences in category totals easy to compare.

**5. Based on the trend, what recommendations would you give management?**  
Plan adequate Electronics inventory for increasing demand, maintain stock for Clothing and Groceries, use monthly sales trends for forecasting, and evaluate profit margins before making profitability claims.
